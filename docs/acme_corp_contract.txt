1. SERVICES

Vendor agrees to provide the following services to Client:

2. TERM

This Agreement shall commence on February 1, 2025, and continue for a period of 24 months, expiring on January 31, 2027, unless earlier terminated.

3. COMPENSATION

Client agrees to pay Vendor the following fees:
- Base monthly retainer: $45,000/month

- Annual price adjustment: CPI + 2%

Total estimated annual contract value: $540,000

4. PAYMENT TERMS

Invoices shall be submitted on the first business day of each month. Payment is due within 30 days of invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality of all proprietary information exchanged during the term of this Agreement and for 3 years thereafter.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All custom software developed under this Agreement shall be owned by Client upon full payment. Vendor retains rights to pre-existing IP and general methodologies.

7. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 60 days written notice. Client may terminate immediately for cause. Early termination by Client without cause requires payment of 3 months remaining fees.

8. LIABILITY LIMITATION

Vendor's total liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the preceding 3 months. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
