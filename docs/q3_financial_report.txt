QUARTERLY FINANCIAL REPORT - Q3 2025
GlobalTech Industries
Confidential - For Internal Use Only

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

GlobalTech Industries reports strong Q3 2025 performance with total revenue of $847.3 million, representing a 23% year-over-year increase. Operating income reached $142.8 million (16.9% margin), while net income grew to $98.4 million, up 31% from Q3 2024.

REVENUE BREAKDOWN BY SEGMENT

1. Cloud Services Division
   - Revenue: $412.5M (48.7% of total)
   - YoY Growth: +38%
   - Key driver: Enterprise SaaS subscriptions up 45%
   - Gross margin: 72%

2. Hardware Products
   - Revenue: $198.7M (23.5% of total)
   - YoY Growth: -3%
   - Key driver: Supply chain normalization
   - Gross margin: 31%

3. Professional Services
   - Revenue: $156.2M (18.4% of total)
   - YoY Growth: +18%
   - Key driver: AI implementation consulting
   - Gross margin: 45%

4. Licensing & Royalties
   - Revenue: $79.9M (9.4% of total)
   - YoY Growth: +12%
   - Gross margin: 89%
